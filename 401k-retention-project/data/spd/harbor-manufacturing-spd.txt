SUMMARY PLAN DESCRIPTION
Harbor Manufacturing 401(k) Savings Plan
Plan Year: January 1 - December 31
Document ID: SPD-HM-2026-01 (SYNTHETIC - fictional plan for demo use)

ELIGIBILITY
Employees of Harbor Manufacturing Co. are eligible to participate on the first day of the month following 60 days of service. Eligibility requires a minimum age of 21. Part-time employees working at least 1,000 hours in a plan year are eligible.

EMPLOYEE CONTRIBUTIONS
Participants may contribute 1% to 75% of eligible compensation on a pre-tax or Roth basis, up to the annual IRS limit. Catch-up contributions are permitted for participants age 50 and older. Contribution rate changes take effect at the start of the next payroll period.

EMPLOYER MATCH
Harbor Manufacturing matches 100% of the first 3% of compensation contributed, plus 50% of the next 2%. Matching contributions are made each payroll period.

VESTING
Employee contributions are always 100% vested. Employer matching contributions vest on a 4-year graded schedule: 25% after 1 year of service, 50% after 2 years, 75% after 3 years, 100% after 4 years.

ROLLOVERS INTO THE PLAN
The plan accepts rollover contributions from prior employer 401(k), 403(b), and governmental 457(b) plans, and from traditional IRAs. Rollover checks must be made payable to the Harbor Manufacturing 401(k) Plan. Rollover contributions are not subject to the plan's waiting period and are immediately vested.

DISTRIBUTIONS AND WITHDRAWALS
Distributions are permitted at termination of employment, retirement, age 59 1/2, disability, or death. Hardship withdrawals are available for qualifying immediate and heavy financial needs as defined by IRS safe harbor rules. In-service withdrawals are not permitted before age 59 1/2.

LOANS
Participants may borrow up to 50% of the vested balance, maximum 50,000 dollars, minimum 1,000 dollars. One outstanding loan at a time. Repayment through payroll deduction over up to 5 years.

FEES
The plan charges a quarterly administrative fee of 12.50 dollars per participant. Investment fund expense ratios range from 0.04% to 0.62%. There is no fee for rollover contributions.

PLAN CONTACT
Harbor Benefits Service Center, 1-800-555-0142, Monday-Friday 8am-8pm ET. Rollover paperwork is processed within 5 business days of receipt.
