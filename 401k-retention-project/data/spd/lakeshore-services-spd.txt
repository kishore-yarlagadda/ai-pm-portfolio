SUMMARY PLAN DESCRIPTION
Lakeshore Services Group 401(k) Retirement Plan
Plan Year: July 1 - June 30
Document ID: SPD-LS-2026-02 (SYNTHETIC - fictional plan for demo use)

ELIGIBILITY
All full-time employees of Lakeshore Services Group are eligible immediately upon hire. There is no minimum service requirement. Eligible employees are auto-enrolled at 3% pre-tax unless they opt out within 30 days of hire.

EMPLOYEE CONTRIBUTIONS
Participants may contribute 1% to 100% of eligible compensation on a pre-tax or Roth basis, up to the annual IRS limit. Catch-up contributions are permitted for participants age 50 and older. Auto-escalation increases contributions by 1% each plan year up to a maximum of 10%, unless the participant opts out.

EMPLOYER CONTRIBUTIONS
Lakeshore Services matches 50% of the first 6% of compensation contributed. The plan also provides a discretionary profit-sharing contribution determined annually, allocated in proportion to compensation.

VESTING
Employee contributions are always 100% vested. Employer matching contributions vest on a 3-year cliff schedule: 0% before 3 years of service, 100% at 3 years. Profit-sharing contributions vest on the same 3-year cliff.

ROLLOVERS INTO THE PLAN
The plan accepts rollover contributions from prior employer 401(k) and 403(b) plans. Rollovers from IRAs are NOT accepted. Rollover contributions are immediately vested and may be requested through the participant portal or by paper form.

DISTRIBUTIONS AND WITHDRAWALS
Distributions are permitted at termination of employment, retirement, age 59 1/2, disability, or death. Hardship withdrawals follow IRS safe harbor rules. In-service withdrawals of rollover contributions are permitted at any age.

LOANS
The plan does not permit participant loans.

FEES
Administrative costs are paid by the employer. Participants pay only investment fund expense ratios, which range from 0.03% to 0.45%. A 25 dollar fee applies to outbound rollover or distribution processing.

PLAN CONTACT
Lakeshore Retirement Services, 1-800-555-0198, Monday-Friday 9am-6pm ET. Outbound rollover requests are processed within 7-10 business days.
